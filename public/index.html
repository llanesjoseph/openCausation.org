OpenCausation.org
Overview

OpenCausation.org is being developed as the educational and methodology-focused home for the OpenCausation concept. It introduces a neutral, evidence-informed approach to understanding medical causation review.

Structured consideration of

Diagnosis Exposure Mechanism Timing Objective findings Individual factors Competing explanations Documentation completeness

The site is intended to support education, transparency, and a more consistent understanding of causation-related review.

It is not intended to provide legal advice, medical treatment advice, or final medical-legal determinations.
