Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

OpenCausation.org is an educational and informational platform. It does not currently provide individualized medical or legal services through the public website.

Privacy at a glance
  • OpenCausation.org uses a privacy-preserving, cookieless web analytics service to understand general site usage. It sets no cookies, does not track individual visitors, and does not build visitor profiles. The site uses no advertising or cross-site tracking technologies.
  • No registration is currently required to access the publicly available educational content.
  • OpenCausation does not currently require users to create an account, log in, or provide identifying information to browse public content.
  • Website infrastructure may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser and device data, access times, server logs, and cookie or security identifiers.
  • Do not submit protected health information, identifiable medical records, privileged communications, or confidential case materials through ordinary email or website contact channels.
  • This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website and its data practices change. Additional notice, consent, or privacy choices will be provided when required by applicable law.

01

Purpose and Scope

OpenCausation.org is operated by Causation.com, Inc., in this Privacy Policy, "OpenCausation," "we," "us," and "our" refer to Causation.com, Inc. This Policy describes how information may be processed when you visit OpenCausation.org or use a website feature that links to this Policy.

This Policy applies only to OpenCausation websites, pages, and features that display or link to it. It does not govern third-party websites, services, embedded content, or resources that maintain their own privacy practices or policies.

This Policy describes OpenCausation's current practices. It may be revised if the website, its features, or its data practices change.

02

Current Public-Access Model

At the date of this Policy, users may access publicly available educational content without registering, creating an account, logging in, or providing a name, email address, professional credential, or other identifying information.

Although registration is not required, website infrastructure and service providers may automatically process limited technical information involved in operating, delivering, securing, and maintaining the website.

OpenCausation does not currently offer individualized medical care, legal representation, professional consultation, or a secure clinical communication portal through the public website. Website content is educational and informational and is not a substitute for advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

03

Personal Information

For purposes of this Policy, "Personal Information" means information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an identifiable individual or household, as defined by applicable law.

Personal Information generally does not include aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify an individual, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

04

Information Processed

The particular technical information processed depends on the website configuration and the service providers used at the time of access. Current categories may include the following:

4.1 Information Not Currently Required

OpenCausation does not currently require a user account, login credentials, profile, subscription, payment information, professional credentials, National Provider Identifier, medical license number, or demographic profile to view public educational content.

4.2 Technical and Usage Information

Website systems and service providers may process information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, language settings, date and time of access, referring and exit pages, requested pages or resources, diagnostic and security events, general location inferred from IP address, and similar server-log or network information.

4.3 Cookies and Similar Identifiers

The website may use essential cookies, local storage, content-delivery identifiers, security tokens, or comparable technologies involved in website operation, security, and user-selected settings.

4.4 Information Voluntarily Provided

If you contact OpenCausation for an administrative, privacy, copyright, legal, or other permitted purpose, we may receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, organization, message, and attachments. OpenCausation does not currently solicit case materials, individualized medical or legal questions, general content submissions, or confidential information through ordinary email.

4.5 Third-Party Features

If the website includes embedded videos, maps, social-media tools, external fonts, or other third-party content, those providers may receive technical information when the content loads or when you interact with it.

05

How Information Is Used

OpenCausation may use information, to the extent relevant, for the following purposes:

• To deliver public website pages, educational content, and requested resources;

• To operate, maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and protect the website and its infrastructure;

• To detect, investigate, and prevent misuse, fraud, malicious activity, security incidents, or violations of applicable terms;

• To evaluate and improve website performance, accessibility, navigation, content, and functionality when relevant tools are used;

• To respond to communications, questions, support requests, or feedback;

• To comply with law, valid legal process, regulatory obligations, and enforceable governmental requests;

• To establish, exercise, or defend legal rights and protect the rights, safety, property, or security of OpenCausation, users, or others; and

• For other purposes disclosed when information is collected or with the individual's direction or consent.

OpenCausation processes information for the purposes described in this Policy and as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

06

How Information May Be Disclosed

OpenCausation may disclose information as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or as otherwise permitted or required by law. Categories of recipients may include:

Service providers. Website hosting, content delivery, security, analytics, email, support, storage, and other vendors may process information to perform services for OpenCausation, subject to applicable law and the arrangements governing those services.

Legal and safety recipients. Information may be disclosed when reasonably believed necessary to comply with law or valid legal process; respond to lawful requests; investigate fraud or misuse; protect safety, security, property, or legal rights; or enforce agreements.

Business transfers. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, acquisition, bankruptcy, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law.

At your direction or with consent. Information may be disclosed when you request or authorize the disclosure.

Aggregated or de-identified information. OpenCausation may use or disclose information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it does not reasonably identify an individual, subject to applicable law.

07

Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

Current status: OpenCausation.org uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics service, to measure general site usage. It sets no cookies or browser storage, does not track individual visitors, does not build visitor profiles, and does not use fingerprinting or advertising. The site uses no other analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking technologies; any change will be reflected in this Policy.

Cookies and similar technologies are small files, identifiers, or storage mechanisms that can help websites operate, remember settings, understand performance, and protect against misuse.

7.1 Essential Technologies

OpenCausation and its service providers may use technologies reasonably necessary to deliver pages, balance traffic, maintain security, detect malicious activity, preserve user-selected settings, or support other essential functions.

7.2 Analytics and Similar Technologies

OpenCausation may use analytics or similar technologies to understand website operation and general usage. Any such technologies are subject to the settings, notices, and choices made available on the website and to applicable law.

7.3 Your Choices

You can often control cookies through browser settings and, when available, the website's cookie-preference tool. Blocking some technologies may affect website functionality. OpenCausation will recognize legally required privacy-choice signals when and to the extent applicable law requires it.

08

Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Features

OpenCausation does not currently provide a public interactive feature requiring users to submit questions, medical records, confidential case information, or other materials. If such a feature is introduced, OpenCausation may provide additional disclosures appropriate to the feature and as required by applicable law.

09

Health Information and Confidential Case Information

OpenCausation is a public educational website and is not designed to receive protected health information, identifiable medical records, confidential claim files, attorney-client communications, work product, or other sensitive case materials through ordinary website or email channels.

Unless OpenCausation expressly provides a designated secure feature and separately agrees in writing to receive such information, do not submit names, dates of birth, medical-record numbers, claim numbers, identifiable medical records, or other information that could identify an individual whose condition or claim is being discussed.

Submission of protected health information, identifiable medical records, confidential case materials, privileged communications, or similar sensitive information through an undesignated website or ordinary email channel is unauthorized. Receipt of such information does not, by itself, create a clinician-individual relationship, attorney-client relationship, fiduciary relationship, business-associate relationship, or other special duty of confidentiality beyond duties imposed by applicable law.

OpenCausation does not represent that ordinary website access, ordinary email communications, or general contact tools are secure or constitute HIPAA-compliant communication channels.

Third-party content embedded in a page may allow the third party to receive technical information when the page loads or when you interact with the content. Your use of a third-party service is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy. Review those policies before providing information to the third party.

To the extent permitted by law, OpenCausation is not responsible for the privacy, security, availability, content, or practices of third-party websites or services.

11

Retention and Security

11.1 Data Retention

OpenCausation may retain Personal Information for as long as it determines reasonably necessary or appropriate for the purpose for which it was obtained, website operation and security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, enforcement of agreements, recordkeeping, or other purposes permitted by applicable law.

Information may remain in backup, archival, security, or system records for additional periods consistent with operational needs and applicable law.

OpenCausation may retain aggregated or de-identified information for research, statistical, security, or improvement purposes when the information no longer reasonably identifies an individual and is handled in accordance with applicable law.

11.2 Data Security and Incident Response

OpenCausation uses safeguards it considers reasonable and appropriate in light of the nature of the information it processes, the website's functions, and reasonably foreseeable risks. No method of internet transmission, website operation, or electronic storage is completely secure, and OpenCausation does not guarantee absolute security, uninterrupted availability, or prevention of every unauthorized access attempt or incident.

If OpenCausation identifies a security incident involving Personal Information, it will investigate and provide notifications when and as required by applicable law.

12

Applicable Privacy Requests

OpenCausation will respond to requests concerning Personal Information as required by applicable law. Because OpenCausation currently does not require registration or user accounts, the Personal Information maintained about a visitor may be limited to information voluntarily provided to OpenCausation and technical information processed in connection with the operation, security, and use of the website.

To ask a privacy question or submit a request concerning Personal Information, contact us at info@causation.com. OpenCausation may take reasonable steps to verify the identity and authority of the person making the request. OpenCausation may deny, limit, or decline a request, or retain information, when permitted or required by applicable law.

Privacy rights and request procedures vary by jurisdiction. Any rights described or exercised under this section apply only when the relevant law applies to OpenCausation and to the particular information or processing activity involved.

OpenCausation does not currently sell Personal Information or use it for targeted advertising.

13

Children's Privacy

OpenCausation is a general-audience educational website and is not directed to children under 13. OpenCausation does not knowingly collect Personal Information online from a child under 13 in a manner subject to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act without required parental notice and consent.

If OpenCausation learns that it has collected Personal Information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, it will take steps concerning the information as required by applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes that a child has provided Personal Information may contact OpenCausation using the contact information below.
