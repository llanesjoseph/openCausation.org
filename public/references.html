OpenCausation.orgSM is an independent, open, noncommercial educational project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of the American Medical Association. The site provides general educational information only and does not provide medical advice, legal advice, or case-specific causation or apportionment opinions. Causation determinations require an objective diagnosis, complete records, clinical judgment, exposure analysis, reliable scientific evidence, consideration of alternative explanations, and application of the legal standards of the relevant jurisdiction. Legal standards vary by jurisdiction and may change over time; the controlling law of the relevant forum governs any actual matter. Examples and illustrations are hypothetical and educational only. This resource is grounded in established causation-analysis principles, including the AMA Guides to the Evaluation of Disease and Injury Causation, 2nd ed. (2014), occupational and epidemiologic methods, and other scientific resources relevant to evidence-based causation analysis.
